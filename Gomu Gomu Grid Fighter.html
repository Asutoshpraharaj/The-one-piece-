Thin orange lines and rings are about to hit you, so step off them before they flash. Attacks auto-aim at the nearest enemy, or at whoever you are moving toward. Hit an enemy during its windup to cancel the strike. Brutes and bosses are armored. Chain hits to build a combo that raises your damage.
